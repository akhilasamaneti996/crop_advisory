Agronomic Best Practices
Morning Irrigation

Irrigating early between 6:00 AM and 8:30 AM minimizes water loss from wind and high sun evaporation.

Soil Testing Regularity

Test soil NPK and organic carbon every 2-3 years before major sowing to prevent excessive fertilizer runoff.

Preventive Scouting

Inspect the lower canopy leaves once every 4 days. Most foliar blights begin on mature bottom foliage.
